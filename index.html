More about me

How I got here

I started in mechanical engineering, but a financial mathematics course changed my direction. We built a project that optimized options trading strategies, and I enjoyed that data and modeling work more than anything in my core courses. So I went to NYU for an MS in Management of Technology, focused on data science and product. There I learned that knowing what to build and why matters as much as knowing how to build it.

From data to product

At Optum I started on data problems in Workforce Management. I moved into product management so I could use data to drive decisions instead of only supporting them. My first real call was an AI assistant. Data showed developers losing about 80 hours a week searching for APIs and documentation. We built a RAG assistant on GPT-4o and shipped an MVP in 6 months that 40 customers adopted.

Why I love this work

What I enjoy most is deciding what will create the most value for users, engineers and the business. It is rarely obvious, and that is the appeal. I bring a data background to the call, so my priorities come from evidence rather than habit or opinion.

Denial triage and appeal agent

AI agent design and evaluation

Hospitals lose money on denied insurance claims. I built an agent that decides which denials are worth fixing, appealing or letting go, and drafts appeal letters in which every fact cites its source.

99.1%of recoverable dollars kept
94%of denial reasons found

Key outcomes

  • Beat the best approach without AI, which kept 82.5%, on 100 claims held back until the end
  • Picked the smaller model after the larger one named every cause and still lost the most money

  • Called it ready for a supervised pilot, not for running alone, and showed why
